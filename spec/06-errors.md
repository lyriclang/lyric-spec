# Errors

> **Skeleton.** Written with milestone **M5** of the Lyric 5 plan, spec-first: each rule lands
> here with its conformance case before or with its implementation. Source of the decisions:
> [05 Errors](https://github.com/lyriclang/lyric/blob/main/design/v5/spec/05-fehler.md), [01 Runtime L5](https://github.com/lyriclang/lyric/blob/main/design/v5/spec/01-laufzeit.md).

## Scope

`throws` sets, `try`/`try?`/`try!`, `catch`, `Error` and `Exception`, `defer` and `using`, panics, `never`.
