# Interfaces and abstraction

> **Skeleton.** Written with milestone **M4** of the Lyric 5 plan, spec-first: each rule lands
> here with its conformance case before or with its implementation. Source of the decisions:
> [04 Abstraction](https://github.com/lyriclang/lyric/blob/main/design/v5/spec/04-abstraktion.md), [03 Type system](https://github.com/lyriclang/lyric/blob/main/design/v5/spec/03-typsystem.md).

## Scope

Interfaces, `Self`, static members, associated types, conformance and its synthesis, generic `extend`, delegation `by`, operators as interfaces, overloading by arity, named arguments.
